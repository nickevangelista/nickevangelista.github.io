Nícolas Evangelista is a computer engineering student at the State University of Rio Grande do Sul (UERGS), in southern Brazil, and works in software at Infinilabs.

He writes about computing and its consequences: what new tools change in the way people work, think, and decide. Alongside the technical pieces, he publishes reading notes on neuroscience, philosophy, and books. Those are written as notes from a reader, not lessons from an expert.

## What you'll find here

- **Technical notes:** things he built, bugs he fixed, numbers he measured.
- **Reading notes:** what he took from a book or paper, and where he got stuck.
- **Essays:** longer pieces, when a subject needs the room.

## Selected projects

- [AI Council CLI](https://github.com/nickevangelista/aicouncil-cli): a council of AI models in the terminal.
- [Physics Teacher SLM](https://github.com/nickevangelista/physics-teacher-slm): a small language model fine-tuned to teach physics, with a RAG pipeline and a chat interface.
- [Uni-Tracker](https://github.com/nickevangelista/uni-tracker-app): semester planning for UERGS students.
